Chicago roots. Arkansas home.

A Chicago pizza moved to the Ozarks, made some local friends, and started putting sorghum on things.

{{/* 2. THE PIZZA THAT TRAVELED BAND */}}

The Pizza
That Traveled

It came down the interstate in a back seat: one seasoned steel pan, a jar of giardiniera, and a recipe card with “NO KETCHUP” underlined twice. Six hundred miles later it met sorghum, okra, and a cornmeal crust, and it never went home.

Local Spirit.
Lasting Flavor.

We borrow from the neighbors: rice from Stuttgart, pink tomatoes from the south end of the state, sorghum from the hills, and chocolate gravy from every grandmother who ever refused to share the recipe. We return the pans washed.

What Makes
